The Lenses

The Frame Is Only Half the Equation

A beautiful frame is what people see. The lenses are what you see through. Put an ordinary lens in an exceptional frame and you still have an ordinary pair of glasses. That is why every pair we make gets ZEISS lenses, precise measurements, and a professional fit.

Made for Your Prescription

Every ZEISS lens is made to order for your prescription, then sent to us. Nothing comes off a shelf.

Measured for Your Face and Frame

Where your pupils sit, how the frame tilts, and how far it sits from your eyes. These measurements tell the lens exactly where to put your clearest vision.

Chosen for How You Live

Screens all day, driving at night, sun on the water. We match the lens design and coatings to your day, not the other way around.

Cut and Mounted in Our Lab

We edge your lenses and mount them in your frame right here in Kailua, so we control the fit from start to finish.

The Process

How Buying Glasses Works at Out to See

Great glasses take a little more care than picking a frame off a wall. Here is exactly what happens, from the first try on to years after you take them home.

  1. 1

    Come In and Try On Anytime

    Walk in any time we are open. Try on as many frames as you like. Our Optician and Dr. Lauren Mordue are in 6 days a week and can always assist. We will tell you honestly what suits your face, your style, and your prescription.

  2. 2

    Bring a Prescription, or Get One Here

    Use a current prescription from any eye doctor, or book a comprehensive eye exam with Dr. Lauren. Your lenses are only as good as the prescription behind them.

  3. 3

    We Measure You in Your Frame

    Once you choose a frame, we adjust it to your face and take precise measurements with it on. This is the step most people never see, and the one that matters most for clear, comfortable vision.

  4. 4

    Choose Your ZEISS Lenses

    We walk you through the lens designs and coatings that fit your prescription and your day, and give you the full price before anything is ordered. No surprises.

  5. 5

    Review and Sign Your Order

    You review everything and sign your order on our iPad. Because every pair is made just for you, an order cannot be changed once it is placed, so we take our time here and answer every question first.

  6. 6

    ZEISS Makes Your Lenses. We Finish Them.

    ZEISS makes your lenses to order and sends them to us. We cut them, mount them in your frame, and check them against your prescription in our lab. Most glasses are ready in 1 to 2 weeks. Specialty lenses can take a little longer, and we will give you a timeline when you order.

  7. 7

    Pick Up and Final Fit

    We fit the finished pair to your face and make sure your vision is sharp and comfortable before you leave.

  8. 8

    For as Long as You Own Them

    Adjustments, nose pad replacements, and minor repairs are always free. Stop by any time something feels off.

Common Questions

Buying Glasses at Out to See: Your Questions, Answered

Shopping for Frames

Do I need an appointment to shop for frames?

No. Walk in any time we are open: Monday to Saturday 10am to 6pm, and Sunday 10am to 3pm. If you would like dedicated time with Cory, text us at (808) 501-2020 and we will set a time.

Can I use a prescription from a different eye doctor?

Absolutely. Bring a current prescription from any provider and use it for frames and lenses from our boutique. We cut and fit your lenses in our own lab no matter where your exam was done.

Can I buy frames without a prescription?

Yes. Every frame in our boutique is available without a prescription. Choose plano lenses with no correction, readers, or just the frame.

Do you have frames for smaller faces or lower nose bridges?

Yes. We stock frames in smaller sizes and with lower bridge fits, a real need for many people in Hawaii. Many of our titanium frames also have adjustable nose pads that we shape to your nose, so the frame sits where your lenses need it.

Are LINDBERG frames worth it?

For many people, yes. LINDBERG frames are made from titanium, weigh very little, and use a screwless hinge with no screws to work loose. Fitted well, they are comfortable enough to forget you are wearing them. The best way to decide is to try one on next to a heavier frame. See our LINDBERG collection.

What makes your selection different from a chain optical store?

We curate independently. Every brand we carry was chosen because we believe in it, not because of a corporate buying agreement. You will find independent makers, European designers, and heritage brands that chain stores do not carry, and every pair of everyday glasses gets ZEISS lenses cut in our own lab.

Can you put new lenses in a frame I already own?

In most cases, yes! We’ll check your frame first to make sure it’s in good condition and suited to your prescription. We’ll then order your custom ZEISS lenses and cut them to fit your frame right here in our lab. The exception is Maui Jim, which only makes its own prescription lenses. Best of all, you can keep wearing your glasses while your new lenses are being made. Once the lenses arrive, you’ll only need to leave your frame with us for a few hours while we cut, mount, and inspect your new lenses.

Do you carry prescription sunglasses and sports eyewear?

Yes. We carry polarized sunglasses made for Hawaii light, plus sports and safety glasses, and most can be made with your prescription. See our sunglasses.

Lenses and Your Order

What lenses do you use?

We use ZEISS lenses. Each pair is made to order for your prescription, then cut and mounted in our lab. Maui Jim prescription sunglasses are the exception. Maui Jim makes those lenses in its own lab and ships the pair complete. Learn about our ZEISS lens options.

How long does it take to get my glasses?

Most glasses are ready in around 2 weeks, give or take. ZEISS makes your lenses to order, then we cut, mount, and carefully inspect them in our in-house lab. Maui Jim prescription eyewear arrives complete from their lab and is often ready in about a week. Complex prescriptions, custom tints, special-order frames, and other custom requests may take additional time. We’ll let you know if we expect your order to take longer than usual.

Can I change or cancel my order after it is placed?

Once an eyewear order has been placed, it cannot be canceled, as our lenses are custom made specifically for you. However, if your new eyewear isn’t working as expected, don’t worry! We have a remake period that allows us to make adjustments to things like your prescription, measurements, lens design, or lens options to help get everything just right.

What if my new glasses do not feel right?

Come back and see us. Most issues come down to fit, and adjustments are always free. If it is something else, we will check your measurements and prescription and work out the right fix with you.

Do you repair and adjust glasses I bought somewhere else?

Yes, and it is always free. Frame adjustments, nose pad replacements, and minor repairs are complimentary no matter where you bought your glasses.

Payment and Insurance

Do you accept HSA and FSA?

Yes. Prescription glasses and lenses are an eligible expense for most HSA and FSA plans. If you are unsure about yours, check with your plan administrator.

Do you take vision insurance?

We accept UHA. We do not accept VSP or EyeMed, which lets us spend more time with every patient and choose our frames and lenses freely. Some plans reimburse you for out of network purchases, so it is worth checking with yours. See pricing and insurance.

How much do glasses cost?

It depends on the frame and lenses you choose. We will always give you the full price before anything is ordered. Exam and service prices are on our pricing page.

What forms of payment do you accept?

HSA and FSA cards, major credit cards, and yes, Bitcoin.
