The Lenses

ZEISS lenses, made for one person: you

A great frame with an ordinary lens is still an ordinary pair of glasses. That's why we fit ZEISS. Each lens is created individually for your prescription and eyes, almost like a fingerprint, and arrives as a larger disc. Our optician then cuts and fits it to your exact frame, right here in the shop.

  • ZEISS ClearMind in single vision, anti fatigue, and progressive designs, with wider, clearer progressive zones
  • PhotoFusion lenses that darken outside and clear up fast indoors
  • DuraVision Plus coatings that resist scratches, glare, and smudges
  • New lenses in your own frames. If you love the frame you have, we can put new ZEISS lenses in it
Buy Once

A pair you'll keep for years

Cheaper frames get replaced every year or two. Well made titanium and screwless frames can last for many years, so you buy the frame once and simply update the lenses as your prescription changes.

Take your time trying them on. Our full time optician will help you find what fits your face and your life, and adjustments are always free.

The Eye Exam

And an eye doctor who gives you a full hour

Great glasses start with an accurate prescription. Every comprehensive exam with Dr. Lauren Mordue, OD, is booked for a full hour. Dr. Mordue trained at the New England College of Optometry in Boston and sees patients of all ages, from keiki to kupuna. Every exam includes, with no upcharges:

  • Digital refraction
  • Optomap widefield retinal imaging
  • OCT scans of the macula and optic nerve
  • Eye pressure testing and corneal topography

Yes. Our in house lens lab and optician can fit new ZEISS lenses into many frames you already own. The exception is Maui Jim, which only makes its own prescription lenses, so we order those pairs from Maui Jim.
