Three generations, one obsession with color

David Pellicer founded Etnia Barcelona in 2001, but the story starts earlier. His grandfather opened an eyewear factory in the 1950s, and his father grew the family business across Spain in the 1980s.

When most eyewear came in black or brown, Etnia Barcelona built its name on color, inspired by the art, architecture and energy of Barcelona.

What makes Etnia Barcelona different

Natural acetate

Frames made from Mazzucchelli acetate, derived from natural cotton and wood fibers.

Hundreds of colors

A palette of more than 500 color references, inspired by fashion and art.

Art collaborations

Collections created with artists, so some frames carry a story as well as a style.

Questions about Etnia Barcelona

How do I choose the right color?

Try a few in natural light. Colors look very different on different skin tones and hair, and we are happy to give an honest opinion.

Can Etnia Barcelona sunglasses be made in my prescription?

Yes. We fit them with ZEISS prescription sun lenses, polarized if you like.

Is natural acetate durable?

Yes. It is strong and comfortable, and it can be adjusted for a precise fit. We fine tune it at pickup and any time after.

Do I need an eye exam first?

Bring a current prescription, or book a full hour eye exam with Dr. Mordue and choose your frames the same visit.
