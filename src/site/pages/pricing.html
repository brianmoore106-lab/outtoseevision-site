Insurance & Payment

Your Payment Options

We work with several insurance plans and offer flexible ways to pay. Questions? Just give us a call — we're happy to help figure out what works for you.

Tricare Insurance

We accept Tricare Prime and Tricare Select plans as a non-network provider. Tricare Prime covers exams every 12 months for active duty plans and every 24 months for retiree plans. Tricare Select covers exams every 12 months for active duty plans. Tricare Select Retiree plans do not cover routine eye exams — there may be a supplemental vision plan.

Active duty members and their families receive 10% off services, prescription glasses, non-prescription sunglasses, and contact lenses. Military ID must be present.

Military & Family Discount Available
UHA Insurance

We are in network with UHA insurance — that's University Health Alliance, not United Health Care.

If you're unsure whether your plan qualifies, give us a call and we'll help you figure it out before your visit.

FSA / HSA Funds

You are always welcome to use your FSA or HSA funds at Out to See — for exams, eyewear, contact lenses, and more.

These accounts are a great way to maximize your benefits before they expire at year's end.

Out-of-Network Claims

For many other vision plans, you may still receive out-of-network reimbursement — giving you the freedom to choose the provider you trust.

Our team is happy to assist with the submission process, or we can provide an itemized receipt so you can file directly with your insurance.

CareCredit Financing

We offer financing through CareCredit on purchases over $200. Enjoy 12 months of deferred interest — no interest as long as your balance is paid in full within 12 months.

Checking to pre-qualify is quick, easy, and has no impact on your credit score.

Bitcoin, Credit Card & Cash

We proudly accept Bitcoin via Cash App at OUT TO SEE. You don't even need Bitcoin to pay in Bitcoin — Cash App connects to your checking account and uses the Bitcoin Lightning Network, skipping the 3–4% fees of traditional credit cards.

We also accept all major credit cards and cash.

What's Included

Our Services

No surprise upcharges. Every standard test is included in your exam fee — because good care shouldn't come with hidden costs.

Comprehensive Eye Exam
Out to See provides a full eye health evaluation including Optomap widefield retinal imaging, screening of the macula and optic nerve with OCT technology, measurement of eye pressure, topographical measurements of the cornea, and digital prescription testing — all included. No upcharges for routine tests, no “pay extra to skip dilation.” Just thorough, complete care.
Contact Lens Exam
Includes a comprehensive eye exam plus a contact lens fitting. If we have the right trial lenses in office, we can often do the fitting the same day. The fitting process includes any necessary follow-up visits until the best fit is achieved. New wearers are taught how to insert, remove, and care for their lenses. Pricing varies depending on fitting complexity (astigmatism or multifocal contacts).
Urgent Eye Care & Office Visits
For individuals who have sustained an eye injury or are experiencing symptoms such as redness, pain, or discharge. Keep in mind that not all conditions require a prescription to treat — some can actually worsen with medication. Follow-up appointments may be necessary; charges for follow-up visits will vary depending on the complexity of the visit.
