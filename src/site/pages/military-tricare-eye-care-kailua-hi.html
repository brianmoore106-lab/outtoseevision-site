How TRICARE works at Out To See

We accept TRICARE Prime and TRICARE Select as a non-network provider. Here's the quick version of what's covered:

  • TRICARE Prime — routine eye exam every 12 months (active duty) or every 24 months (retiree).
  • TRICARE Select — routine eye exam every 12 months (active duty).
  • TRICARE Select Retiree — routine eye exams aren't covered, but you may have a supplemental vision plan that helps.
  • FSA / HSA — always accepted for exams, eyewear, and contacts.

Plans and eligibility vary — call or text (808) 501-2020 before your visit and we'll confirm exactly what your benefits cover, no surprises at checkout.

Military & TRICARE FAQ

Do you accept TRICARE?

Yes — we accept TRICARE Prime and TRICARE Select as a non-network provider. Coverage varies by plan and status, so call or text (808) 501-2020 and we'll confirm your specific benefits before your visit.

Is there a military discount?

Yes. Active-duty members and their families receive 10% off services, prescription glasses, non-prescription sunglasses, and contact lenses. Just have your military ID with you.

How close are you to Marine Corps Base Hawaii?

We're in Kailua at Lau Hala Shops — a short, easy drive from MCBH in Kāneʻohe Bay, with free parking right behind the building.

Do I need a referral?

For a routine eye exam, generally no. If you're unsure how your plan works, give us a call and we'll walk you through it.

Can I use my FSA or HSA?

Absolutely — exams, glasses, and contacts are all FSA/HSA-eligible, so you can use pre-tax dollars toward your visit and eyewear.

Do you see children and teens?

Yes — we see patients of all ages, including keiki. We keep kids' exams calm and friendly, which makes us a great fit for the whole family.
