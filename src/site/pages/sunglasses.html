The Hawaii UV Problem

Your eyes absorb more UV here than almost anywhere else in the U.S.

Most people treat sunglasses as a comfort purchase. In Hawaii, they're a health decision. UV intensity here is categorically different from the mainland.

☀️
11+

Extreme UV Index

Hawaii's UV Index regularly hits 11 or higher in summer — classified as "Extreme." The CDC considers anything over 8 a serious health risk.

🌊
25%

Water Reflects UV Upward

Water reflects up to 25% of UV back at your eyes from below — meaning you're hit from two angles simultaneously. Polarized lenses block this.

🌐
20°N

Latitude Makes It Worse

Kailua is dramatically closer to the equator than Los Angeles or New York. UV intensity increases significantly as latitude decreases.

"A dark lens without UV protection can actually be more dangerous than no sunglasses — your pupils dilate behind the tint, allowing more radiation to reach the retina."— American Optometric Association
