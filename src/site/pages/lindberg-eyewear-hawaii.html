Since 1983

Made in Denmark, still

The short version, from LINDBERG's own history. Every pair is handmade to order.

Aarhus

The first air titanium prototype was made in 1983. In 1986 it went on sale, and Poul-Jørn Lindberg founded the company with his son Henrik, an architect. The frames are still crafted in Denmark.

No screws

The hinge is a spiral of titanium. No screws, no rivets, nothing extra to loosen. Air titanium, designed with the architects Dissing+Weitling, was the first rimless frame made of titanium.

Almost nothing

Titanium is ultra-lightweight, flexible, durable, and hypoallergenic. The lightest spirit titanium frames are 1.9 grams. n.o.w. frames can be as little as 2.3 grams.

Yours

Color, size, temple length, and nose pads can be chosen. Titanium frames come in 36 colors, and the front, temples, and temple covers can be combined. The Danish ID Prize came in 1989. LINDBERG now holds 114 major design awards.
