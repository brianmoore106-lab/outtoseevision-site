Four decades of Danish restraint

LINDBERG began in 1983, when optician Poul Jørn Lindberg and his wife Hanne set out to make glasses that did less, better. In 1986, working with architect Hans Dissing, they introduced a titanium wire frame with a hinge made from the wire itself. No screws to loosen. No solder to crack.

That idea still defines the brand today, and it has earned LINDBERG a long list of international design awards.

Are LINDBERG frames worth it?
