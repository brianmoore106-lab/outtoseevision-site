What the hour looks like

No countdown clock. Here's how it goes.

:00

Imaging first, not guesswork

Before you ever squint at a chart, we photograph the back of your eye. Retinal imaging, eye pressure, peripheral vision — a real picture of your eye health, captured in the first few minutes.

:10

The chair, with Dr. Mordue

One-on-one, start to finish. Digital refraction replaces the old "one or two?" guessing game, and there's time for the questions you've been saving — all of them.

:35

Your eyes, on screen, explained

Dr. Mordue walks you through your own scans in plain language — what she sees, what it means, what to watch. You leave actually understanding your eyes, maybe for the first time.

:50

The frame wall & the lab

If you need glasses, this is the fun part. Lindberg, Moscot, Mykita, Krewe — frames chosen by hand, ZEISS lenses cut in our own lab by Cory, our master lens maker, and adjusted until they sit just right.
