Before You Arrive

Parking Guide

Parking is free — but there are a few things worth knowing before you pull in.

Free Parking
Parking is free at Lau Hala Shops. The main lot is located behind the building. There's also a garage near Longs Drugs if the main lot is full.
3-Hour Limit
Parking is limited to three hours and is strictly enforced. Please keep an eye on your time, especially if you have a longer appointment or plan to shop around.
Curbside Pickup
There are a few curbside pickup stalls directly in front of our store. These are great for quick frame or contact lens pickups without circling the lot.
Thursday Farmer's Market Warning
On Thursday afternoons, a portion of the parking lot and garage are reserved for the farmer's market. You will be towed if parked in those areas after 2pm. Trust us — it happened to Dr. Mordue at 2:02pm.
Getting Here

Directions to Our Office

Coming from Honolulu, Kaneohe, Waimanalo, or Hawaii Kai — here's how to find us at Lau Hala Shops in Kailua.

Via Pali Highway (HI-61)
From Honolulu & H-1
Take the Nuʻuanu Pali Highway (HI-61) northeast from Honolulu. Drive through the Pali Tunnels and descend into Kailua. Continue straight on Kailua Road into town — Lau Hala Shops will be on your right. Look for the former Macy's building.
Via H-3 Freeway
From H-3 & MCBH
Take H-3 east and exit onto Mokapu Blvd heading toward Kailua. Follow Mokapu Blvd as it becomes Kailua Road and continues into downtown. Lau Hala Shops will be on your left as you reach the town center.
Via Likelike Highway (HI-63)
From Kaneohe
From Kaneohe, take Kailua Road (HI-61) south toward Kailua town. Continue through the roundabout and follow Kailua Road until you reach Lau Hala Shops on your right. Alternatively, take Kahekili Highway (HI-83) south to connect with Kailua Road.
Via Kalanianaʻole Hwy (HI-72)
From Waimanalo & Hawaii Kai
Take Kalanianaʻole Highway (HI-72) north through Waimanalo toward Kailua. Turn left onto Kailua Road at the intersection heading into downtown. Lau Hala Shops will be on your right — Suite 106, next to Maui Brewing Company.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Where exactly is Out to See Vision Care located?
We're at 573 Kailua Road, Suite 106, inside Lau Hala Shops — the former Macy's building in downtown Kailua. You'll find us next door to Maui Brewing Company and facing Ulta. If you're walking in from the parking lot, look for our sign near the Kailua Road entrance.
Is parking free at Lau Hala Shops?
Yes — parking is completely free. The main lot is behind the building, and there's additional parking in the garage near Longs Drugs. Parking is limited to three hours and is strictly enforced, so plan accordingly for longer visits.
What is the Thursday parking situation at Lau Hala Shops?
On Thursday afternoons, a portion of the parking lot and the garage are reserved for the Kailua Farmer's Market. If you're parked in those areas after 2pm, you will be towed. Always read the posted signs when you arrive — and trust us on this one, Dr. Mordue learned the hard way at 2:02pm.
What are your hours?
We're open Monday through Saturday from 10am to 6pm, and Sunday from 10am to 3pm. Same-day appointments are often available — just give us a call at (808) 501-2020 or book online.
Is Out to See accessible for patients with mobility needs?
Yes. Lau Hala Shops has accessible parking stalls in the main lot. Our suite is on the ground floor with no stairs required. If you have specific accessibility needs, please call us ahead of your visit and we'll make sure everything is arranged for you.
Do you serve patients from outside Kailua?
Absolutely. While we're based in Kailua, we care for patients from all across O'ahu who are looking for personalized eye care and premium eyewear. We even have patients who travel from the neighbor islands to see us for their eye exams and glasses.
Is there somewhere to eat or shop while I wait?
Definitely. Maui Brewing Company is right next door, and Lau Hala Shops has a variety of restaurants and retailers. Downtown Kailua is also just steps away — one of Oʻahu's best small-town shopping and dining scenes.
