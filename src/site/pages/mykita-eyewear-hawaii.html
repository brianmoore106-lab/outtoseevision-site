Engineered in Berlin

MYKITA was founded in Berlin in 2003 by four friends who wanted to rethink how glasses are made. Instead of outsourcing, they built their own manufactory, known as MYKITA HAUS, where every frame is still assembled by hand.

Their first collection introduced a patented hinge made from the steel itself, with no screws and no welded joints. It became the brand's calling card.

What makes a MYKITA different

The hinge

Flat stainless steel folded into a spring hinge. Nothing to tighten, nothing to fall out.

MYLON

A polyamide material introduced in 2010 and shaped layer by layer with laser sintering. Extremely light, strong, with a soft matte feel.

Serious collaborators

MYKITA has partnered with brands like Leica Camera, which says a lot about the engineering involved.

Handmade

Every frame is assembled by hand in Berlin, not on an assembly line.

Precision frames deserve precision lenses

MYKITA frames are built to tight tolerances, and the lenses should be too. Our lens maker, Cory, measures your eyes in the frame you choose, then cuts ZEISS lenses to fit it exactly in our lab here in Kailua.

The result is a pair that looks sharp and sees sharp.

Questions about MYKITA

Why are MYKITA glasses so light?

Thin stainless steel and MYLON are both very low in weight, and the screwless hinge removes the extra hardware most frames carry.

Can MYKITA sunglasses be made in my prescription?

Most MYKITA frames can take prescription lenses. We fit them with ZEISS sun lenses, polarized where the frame allows.

Are MYKITA frames durable?

The steel hinge is designed to flex without loosening. If anything ever needs adjusting, bring them in and we will take care of it.

Do I need an eye exam first?

Bring a current prescription, or book a full hour eye exam with Dr. Mordue and choose your frames the same visit.
