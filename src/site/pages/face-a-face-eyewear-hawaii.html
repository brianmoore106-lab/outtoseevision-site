Three decades of Parisian nerve

Since 1995, Face à Face has built its name in Paris by ignoring the rules most eyewear follows. Where other brands soften a shape, Face à Face sharpens it. Where others play safe with color, it goes further.

The result is eyewear that works like a signature: instantly personal, and hard to forget. The brand's own motto says it best. Wear your vision.

Questions about Face à Face

Are Face à Face frames too bold for everyday wear?

Most people are surprised how natural they feel once the fit is right. We will help you find a shape that feels like you, not a costume.

Can Face à Face frames take strong prescriptions?

Usually, yes. Some shapes suit higher prescriptions better than others, and we will guide you toward frames and ZEISS lenses that keep things thin and light.

Can they be made into sunglasses?

Many Face à Face optical frames can be fitted with ZEISS prescription sun lenses. Ask us which styles work best.

Do I need an eye exam first?

Bring a current prescription, or book a full hour eye exam with Dr. Mordue and choose your frames the same visit.
