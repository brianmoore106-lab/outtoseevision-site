Walk-in & same-day FAQ

Can I really get an eye exam today?

Often, yes — but it depends entirely on the day's schedule. We hold room for same-day exams whenever we can. The only way to know what's open right now is to call or text (808) 501-2020. If today's full, we'll book you the very next opening — usually within a day or two.

Can I just walk in for an exam?

You're welcome to try, and if there's an opening we'll happily get you seen. But please know exams aren't guaranteed for walk-ins — on a busy day there may not be an opening for several hours. To avoid a wasted trip, call or text first so we can tell you exactly what's available. (Browsing frames, adjustments, and repairs are always walk-in, no appointment needed.)

What should I bring?

Your current glasses or contacts, your insurance card, a photo ID, and a list of any medications. If you have a prior prescription, bring it — but it's not required.

Do you take my insurance?

We're in-network with UHA, and we accept TRICARE Prime and Select as a non-network provider. Your exam is also FSA/HSA eligible. Not sure about your plan? Call or text and we'll help you understand your benefits before you come in.
