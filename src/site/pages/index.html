About Out to See

Not a chain.
Never a rush.

Out to See is an independent, locally owned eye care boutique in the heart of Kailua — not a franchise, not a big-box chain. Dr. Lauren Mordue founded the practice with a simple premise: everyone deserves a thorough exam and a doctor who takes time to explain what they found.

Every appointment is one-on-one with Dr. Mordue. You’ll leave with a full understanding of your vision and eye health — not rushed, not upsold, and not handed off.

Crafted with Obsession

We’re proud to have our own full-service optical lab, led by Master Lens Maker, Cory. Here, we only use ZEISS lenses — the gold standard for unmatched optical clarity.

Every lens is measured, cut, and edged in-house with near-obsessive precision to ensure perfect placement and alignment in your frame. We don’t just assemble glasses — we obsess over the smallest details so your vision is crisp, comfortable, and true.

Advanced Diagnostics
