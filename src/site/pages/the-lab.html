Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What does it mean to have an in-house optical lab?

Most optical practices send your prescription to an off-site lab and wait several weeks for your glasses to return. Our in-house lab means Cory cuts and edges your lenses right here in Kailua. You get faster service, better quality control, and lenses made specifically for your frame by someone who can see the result firsthand.

How long does lens replacement take?

Most lens replacements — whether updating a prescription or replacing scratched lenses — are completed within 1-2 weeks. Complex prescriptions or specialty lenses may take slightly longer; we'll give you an accurate timeline at drop-off.

Can I keep my current frames and just get new lenses?

Yes — this is one of our most popular services. If your frames are in good condition, there's no reason to replace them just because your prescription changed or your lenses got scratched. Bring your frames in and Cory will cut new ZEISS lenses to fit them exactly. The one exception is Maui Jim. Maui Jim only makes its own prescription lenses, so we order those from them.

Do you replace lenses in sunglasses that aren't prescription?

Yes. If your non-prescription sunglass lenses are scratched or damaged, we can often replace them with brand new lenses. This is often much more affordable than buying a new pair — and we can frequently do it the same day. Maui Jim is the exception. Maui Jim only makes its own lenses, so replacement lenses for Maui Jim frames have to come from Maui Jim.

What makes ZEISS lenses worth it?

ZEISS is a global leader in optical innovation, known for setting the standard in lens technology. Their lenses are manufactured to exceptionally strict tolerances, delivering better clarity, sharper edges, and more accurate prescription correction. Their coatings — including anti-reflective, scratch-resistant, and UV-protective — are also more durable. We chose ZEISS exclusively because we believe it's the best available, and we don't want to offer anything less.

Do I need an appointment to have lenses replaced?

For lens replacements using an existing prescription, you can just walk in with your frames. If you need a new prescription first, you'll want to book an eye exam. Call or text us at (808) 501-2020 and we'll get you squared away.
