Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes — we warmly welcome new patients of all ages. You can book online anytime, or call or text us at (808) 501-2020 and we'll get you set up.

Do you offer same-day appointments?

Often, yes! Same-day appointments are frequently available. Call or text (808) 501-2020 or check our online booking page for real-time availability.

Can I text you instead of calling?

Absolutely — texting is welcome! Our number, (808) 501-2020, accepts both calls and texts during regular office hours.

How do I request my prescription or medical records?

You can request records by email at hello@outtoseevision.com, by phone or text at (808) 501-2020, or by fax at (808) 501-2015. Forms are also available through your patient portal.

What do I do if I have an eye emergency?

Call or text us right away at (808) 501-2020. If we're outside office hours and you're experiencing sudden vision loss, a chemical exposure, or serious eye injury, please go to the nearest emergency room immediately.

What insurance do you accept?

We accept UHA and Tricare insurance plans. Visit our Pricing & Insurance page for more information, or call us with any questions about your specific coverage.

How do I find your office?

We're at 573 Kailua Road, Suite 106, inside Lau Hala Shops (the former Macy's building) — right next door to Maui Brewing Company, facing Ulta. Parking is free! See our Location & Parking page for turn-by-turn directions.
