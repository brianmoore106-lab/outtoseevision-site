There are many foods that are good for eye health and can help to maintain good vision. Here are a few examples:

  1. Leafy green vegetables: These vegetables, such as spinach and kale, are rich in nutrients called lutein and zeaxanthin, which can help to protect the eyes from damage caused by blue light and UV radiation.

  2. Citrus fruits: Citrus fruits, such as oranges and grapefruits, are rich in vitamin C, which can help to maintain the health of the eyes and reduce the risk of cataracts.

  3. Fish: Fish, such as salmon, mackerel, and tuna, are rich in omega-3 fatty acids, which can help to reduce the risk of dry eye and other eye conditions.

  4. Nuts and seeds: Nuts and seeds, such as almonds and sunflower seeds, are rich in vitamin E, which can help to protect the eyes from oxidative stress.

  5. Eggs: Eggs are rich in the nutrient lutein, which can help to protect the eyes from damage caused by blue light and UV radiation.

In addition to these foods, it is also important to drink plenty of water and to get enough sleep to help maintain good eye health. A healthy, balanced diet and lifestyle can help to preserve vision and maintain good eye health. If you have specific concerns about your eye health, it is important to see an eye care professional for evaluation and advice.
